How the score works

Each day's score out of 10 starts with the total sunlight forecast for the day, measured against a clear late-spring day in Dromana (about 7.8 kWh per m²). Patchy cloud then takes up to 3 points off, because cloud coming and going makes the charger pause and restart. Storms take off one more. Estimated generation assumes your 13.2 kW of panels turn about 65% of the forecast sunlight into power, allowing for panel angle, heat and the 10 kW inverter limit.

Live data from the controller on the Pi, refreshed every 30 seconds. Solar is read straight from the inverter; house use comes from GoodWe's cloud and runs a few minutes behind. Rates: AGL Night Saver EV, 26.5c peak, 8c off-peak, 1.5c feed-in.
